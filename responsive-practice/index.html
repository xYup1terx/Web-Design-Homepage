Over time, Al is drawn in by how sweet and genuine Kari is. They become really close friends and hangouts start to be just for fun rather than an attempt to gain knowledge. Kari ends up teaching Al a lot about lighter things (pop culture, fun activities, new music). Al finds Kari to be very cool and funny, considering their hangouts to be a fun source of escape from his otherwise boring schedule. They began to look forward to and expect each other's presence. Al finds himself able to let loose around Kari. He appreciates having someone see his habits as a harmless part of him and not an attempt to be harsh or rude. After building a lot of trust, he decides to introduce Kari to his safespace, the underground library. It becomes a recurring thing of theirs to go to that spot at night to hang out.

They have mutual unconfessed crushes on each other. Sadly, the reality of the situation catches up and eventually their relationship is cut short--
